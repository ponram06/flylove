Wires that recruited a neuron

Each row is a real edge from FlyWire FAFB (synapses_nt_v1) whose presynaptic neuron fired before the postsynaptic one in the model. Synapse counts are read from the data file, not typed in.

A toy model, not a biological prediction. The brain is a 14-neuron subgraph of the FlyWire FAFB connectome with real synapse counts; the mapping from arena events to sensory neurons, the neuron dynamics, and the emotion readouts are modelling choices. FAFB is a female brain, while P1 courtship neurons are male-specific (the female counterpart is pC1); cell-type labels come from the data file. Inspired by work on P1/pC1 circuits (Hoopfer et al. 2015, Deutsch et al. 2020). Friend fly: there is no known "friendship neuron" in flies.
